# SALES-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS DASHBOARD
## PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analysis sales data to identify trends in revenue, profit, product performance, and country performance.

## OBJECTIVE
The objective of this project is to analyse sales performance and provide insight that can support better business decision.

## Tools used
-Microsoft excel
-Pivot table
-Pivot charts
-Data cleaning 
-KPI Analysis

## Key performance indicator (KPIs)
-TOTAL REVENUE
-TOTAL PROFIT
-TOTAL COST
-UNITS SOLD
-PROFIT MARGIN

## ANALYSIS PERFORMED
-REVENUE AND PROFIT TREND ANALYSIS
-PRODUCT PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
-COUNTRY PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
-SALES AND PROFIT COMPARISON 

## DASHBOARD
The dashboard provides a visual summary of the company's overall sales performance.

## Key Insights

- Revenue and profit generally increased between 2011 and 2015, with the highest performance occurring around 2015.
- The United States generated the highest revenue among the countries analyzed.
- The overall profit margin was 38%, indicating that the business generated a substantial profit relative to its revenue.
- Product performance varied, with some products generating significantly higher sales than others.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus more resources and marketing efforts on high-performing countries and products.
- Investigate the reasons behind changes in revenue and profit over time.
- Identify low-performing products and evaluate whether they need improved marketing, pricing adjustments, or discontinuation.
- Maintain cost control to protect and potentially improve the overall profit margin.
